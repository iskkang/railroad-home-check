What this service does

Home Surroundings Check presents public transportation and selected environmental records in a format intended for U.S. home buyers and renters. It began with railroad crossing records and expanded to road traffic and optional nearby context. It is independent of government agencies, railroads, property sellers and real-estate brokers.

How information is prepared

Address reports query source services; the report identifies available periods and reporting years. Guides explain how to read those records and link to official source material. Software and AI-assisted tools may help prepare text and code. The service does not imply that an author visited a property, measured sound or inspected a neighborhood.

What has not been independently established

A mapped facility is not proof of a disturbance. A reported incident is not a conviction. Source records can be incomplete or outdated. We do not assign a combined noise, neighborhood safety or investment score, predict indoor decibels, or certify a property’s condition.

Report an error

Use the project issue tracker to flag a broken source, unclear wording or incorrect data interpretation. Include the public page and source reference. Do not post a private address, contact details or other sensitive information. The service cannot alter a government record; corrections to source data may need to go to its publisher.

Advertising and independence

The site includes Google advertising code. Advertising does not establish an endorsement by a data provider. See the privacy policy for analytics and advertising disclosures and data coverage for source limitations.
