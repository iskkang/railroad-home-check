Common questions about trains in Canton

Do trains run at night in Canton?

69 of 69 crossings with reported traffic list trains between 6 PM and 6 AM. Check the specific crossing near a home, because night traffic varies by line.

Are there railroad quiet zones in Canton?

None of the 84 public crossings in this set is reported in an FRA quiet zone, so routine horn sounding is the default at public crossings.

Which railroads operate in Canton?

By number of public crossings: Wheeling & Lake Erie Railway (51), Norfolk Southern (21), Metro Regional Transit Authority (11), Timken (1).

How fast do trains go through Canton?

The highest maximum timetable speed reported at these crossings is 50 mph. Many urban crossings report much lower limits; the busiest-crossing list shows the speed for each.

Is Canton a noisy place to live?

About 4% of Canton residents live where modeled transportation noise averages 55 dB or more over 24 hours (USDOT 2020 noise map), more than the median city among the 299 we cover. Rail is the biggest single source. Noise varies block by block, so check the specific address.

How hot does it get in Canton?

Canton averages about 11 days a year at 90°F or hotter, with summer highs near 83°F (NOAA 1991–2020 normals).

Does it freeze or snow in Canton?

About 120 nights a year drop below freezing, and normal snowfall is 47 inches a year. Winter nights average 23°F.
