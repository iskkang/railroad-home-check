Common questions about trains in Columbia

Do trains run at night in Columbia?

Are there railroad quiet zones in Columbia?

How fast do trains go through Columbia?

How hot does it get in Columbia?

Columbia averages about 35 days a year at 90°F or hotter and 2 at 100°F or hotter, with summer highs near 87°F (NOAA 1991–2020 normals).

Does it freeze or snow in Columbia?
