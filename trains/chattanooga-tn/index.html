Common questions about trains in Chattanooga

Do trains run at night in Chattanooga?

20 of 25 crossings with reported traffic list trains between 6 PM and 6 AM. Check the specific crossing near a home, because night traffic varies by line.

Are there railroad quiet zones in Chattanooga?

None of the 125 public crossings in this set is reported in an FRA quiet zone, so routine horn sounding is the default at public crossings.

Which railroads operate in Chattanooga?

By number of public crossings: Norfolk Southern (85), East Chattanooga Belt Railway (24), CSX (10), Chattooga & Chickamauga Railroad (3).

How fast do trains go through Chattanooga?

The highest maximum timetable speed reported at these crossings is 105 mph. Many urban crossings report much lower limits; the busiest-crossing list shows the speed for each.

Is Chattanooga a noisy place to live?

About 3% of Chattanooga residents live where modeled transportation noise averages 55 dB or more over 24 hours (USDOT 2020 noise map), about the same as the median city among the 299 we cover. Roads are the biggest single source. Noise varies block by block, so check the specific address.

How hot does it get in Chattanooga?

Chattanooga averages about 58 days a year at 90°F or hotter and 2 at 100°F or hotter, with summer highs near 89°F (NOAA 1991–2020 normals).

Does it freeze or snow in Chattanooga?

About 56 nights a year drop below freezing, and normal snowfall is 4 inches a year. Winter nights average 34°F.
