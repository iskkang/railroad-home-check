What this means for a home search in Round Rock

Confirm the crossing, not the neighborhood. Quiet-zone status is reported crossing by crossing (10 of 15 here). Engineers can still sound horns for emergencies, and a home can sit between a quiet-zone crossing and one that is not. How quiet zones work.

Common questions about trains in Round Rock

Do trains run at night in Round Rock?

8 of 8 crossings with reported traffic list trains between 6 PM and 6 AM. Check the specific crossing near a home, because night traffic varies by line.

Are there railroad quiet zones in Round Rock?

10 public crossings are reported in an FRA quiet zone. Status is reported per crossing, so confirm the crossing nearest the address.

Which railroads operate in Round Rock?

By number of public crossings: Union Pacific (15).

How fast do trains go through Round Rock?

The highest maximum timetable speed reported at these crossings is 70 mph. Many urban crossings report much lower limits; the busiest-crossing list shows the speed for each.

How hot does it get in Round Rock?

Round Rock averages about 123 days a year at 90°F or hotter and 29 at 100°F or hotter, with summer highs near 96°F (NOAA 1991–2020 normals).

Does it freeze or snow in Round Rock?

About 12 nights a year drop below freezing, and normal snowfall is 0 inches a year. Winter nights average 44°F.
