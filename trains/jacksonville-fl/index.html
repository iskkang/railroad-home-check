FRA CROSSING DATA · FL

How Many Trains Run Through Jacksonville, FL?

The FRA crossing inventory lists 236 open public at-grade crossings in or near Jacksonville. 109 report at least one through train per day and 99 report trains between 6 PM and 6 AM. On railroad-company lines (excluding transit agencies), a typical active crossing reports about 8 trains per day; the busiest reports 32.

Data retrieved November 9, 2026 from the FRA crossing inventory (latest record update 2026-10-15).

What this means for a home search in Jacksonville

Night trains are the norm, not the exception. 91% of Jacksonville crossings with reported traffic also report nighttime movements. If the home you are considering sits near any active line, plan at least one visit after dark.

No quiet zones appear in this set. None of the 236 public crossings is reported in an FRA quiet zone, so routine horn sounding at public crossings is the default under the federal Train Horn Rule. Distance to the nearest crossing matters more here than in cities with broad quiet-zone coverage.

Most counts are recent. 105 of 109 active crossings report a train-count year of 2021 or later (range 2024–2026). Counts are still reported estimates, not schedules.

Crossing incidents. FRA highway-rail accident reports list 37 incidents at 28 of these crossings between January 2021 and August 2026. That is a safety record for vehicles and pedestrians at the crossing, not a noise measure. How to read crossing accident history.

The busiest railroad-company crossing in this set is Mcquade St (CSX), reporting 19 day and 13 night through trains as of 2024. How to check a home near the tracks in Jacksonville

  1. Look up the address and find the nearest public crossing, not just the nearest track.
  2. Note the day and night counts, quiet-zone status and count year for that crossing ID.
  3. Visit once in daytime and once after 10 PM; stand outside and inside with windows closed.
  4. Ask the seller or neighbors whether traffic has changed since the count year.

Common questions about trains in Jacksonville

Do trains run at night in Jacksonville?

Yes on most active lines: 99 of 109 crossings with reported traffic list trains between 6 PM and 6 AM. Check the specific crossing near a home, because night traffic varies by line.

Are there railroad quiet zones in Jacksonville?

None of the 236 public crossings in this set is reported in an FRA quiet zone, so routine horn sounding is the default at public crossings.

Which railroads operate in Jacksonville?

By number of public crossings: CSX (177), Norfolk Southern (31), Florida East Coast Railway (23), Jacksonville Port Terminal Railroad (5).

How fast do trains go through Jacksonville?

The highest maximum timetable speed reported at these crossings is 79 mph. Many urban crossings report much lower limits; the table above lists the speed for each busy crossing.

Sources and limits

Crossing counts come from the FRA Crossing Inventory (Form 71, current), filtered to open, public, at-grade crossings whose inventory city is Jacksonville (records reported as in or near the city). Incident counts come from FRA highway-rail accident reports (Form 57), January 2021–August 2026. Train counts are railroad-reported estimates with the listed count year; they are not live schedules, noise levels or safety ratings. Some records omit a count year or use placeholder values.
