FRA CROSSING DATA · TX

How Many Trains Run Through Austin, TX?

The FRA crossing inventory lists 83 open public at-grade crossings in or near Austin. 64 report at least one through train per day and 64 report trains between 6 PM and 6 AM. Most of that activity is light rail and commuter service: a typical active crossing reports about 38 trains per day, and the busiest reports 52. Only 10 crossings on railroad-company lines report daily traffic.

Data retrieved November 9, 2026 from the FRA crossing inventory (latest record update 2026-10-05).

What this means for a home search in Austin

Night trains are the norm, not the exception. 100% of Austin crossings with reported traffic also report nighttime movements. If the home you are considering sits near any active line, plan at least one visit after dark.

Quiet zones cover much of the network. 59 of 83 public crossings (71%) are reported in 24-hour quiet zones. Engineers can still sound horns for emergencies and some crossings sit just outside a zone, so confirm the status of the specific crossing nearest the home. How quiet zones work.

The highest counts here come from passenger rail. 62 of the 83 crossings are on transit or commuter lines such as CapMetro. Frequent, shorter trains produce the highest reported counts (up to 52 per day). Railroad-company lines report little traffic in this set, so the table below lists the busiest crossings of any operator.

Most counts are recent. 58 of 64 active crossings report a train-count year of 2021 or later (range 2019–2026). Counts are still reported estimates, not schedules.

Crossing incidents. FRA highway-rail accident reports list 14 incidents at 10 of these crossings between January 2021 and August 2026. That is a safety record for vehicles and pedestrians at the crossing, not a noise measure. How to read crossing accident history.

How to check a home near the tracks in Austin

  1. Look up the address and find the nearest public crossing, not just the nearest track.
  2. Note the day and night counts, quiet-zone status and count year for that crossing ID.
  3. Visit once in daytime and once after 10 PM; stand outside and inside with windows closed.
  4. Ask the seller or neighbors whether traffic has changed since the count year.

Common questions about trains in Austin

Do trains run at night in Austin?

Yes on most active lines: 64 of 64 crossings with reported traffic list trains between 6 PM and 6 AM. Check the specific crossing near a home, because night traffic varies by line.

Are there railroad quiet zones in Austin?

59 public crossings are reported in an FRA quiet zone. Status is reported per crossing, so confirm the crossing nearest the address.

Which railroads operate in Austin?

By number of public crossings: CapMetro (62), Union Pacific (21).

How fast do trains go through Austin?

The highest maximum timetable speed reported at these crossings is 70 mph. Many urban crossings report much lower limits; the table above lists the speed for each busy crossing.

Sources and limits

Crossing counts come from the FRA Crossing Inventory (Form 71, current), filtered to open, public, at-grade crossings whose inventory city is Austin (records reported as in or near the city). Incident counts come from FRA highway-rail accident reports (Form 57), January 2021–August 2026. Train counts are railroad-reported estimates with the listed count year; they are not live schedules, noise levels or safety ratings. Some records omit a count year or use placeholder values.
