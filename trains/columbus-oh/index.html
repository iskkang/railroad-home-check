What this means for a home search in Columbus

Common questions about trains in Columbus

Do trains run at night in Columbus?

31 of 41 crossings with reported traffic list trains between 6 PM and 6 AM. Check the specific crossing near a home, because night traffic varies by line.

Are there railroad quiet zones in Columbus?

None of the 59 public crossings in this set is reported in an FRA quiet zone, so routine horn sounding is the default at public crossings.

Which railroads operate in Columbus?

By number of public crossings: Norfolk Southern (30), CSX (13), Camp Chase Railway (6), Columbus & Ohio River Railroad (4).

How fast do trains go through Columbus?

The highest maximum timetable speed reported at these crossings is 60 mph. Many urban crossings report much lower limits; the busiest-crossing list shows the speed for each.

How hot does it get in Columbus?

Columbus averages about 21 days a year at 90°F or hotter and 0 at 100°F or hotter, with summer highs near 84°F (NOAA 1991–2020 normals).

Does it freeze or snow in Columbus?

About 103 nights a year drop below freezing, and normal snowfall is 28 inches a year. Winter nights average 25°F.
