What this means for a home search in Oklahoma City

Confirm the crossing, not the neighborhood. Quiet-zone status is reported crossing by crossing (6 of 125 here). Engineers can still sound horns for emergencies, and a home can sit between a quiet-zone crossing and one that is not. How quiet zones work.

Crossing incidents. FRA accident reports list 14 highway-rail incidents at 11 of these crossings between January 2021 and August 2026. That is a safety record for vehicles and pedestrians, not a noise measure. How to read crossing accident history.

Common questions about trains in Oklahoma City

Do trains run at night in Oklahoma City?

70 of 74 crossings with reported traffic list trains between 6 PM and 6 AM. Check the specific crossing near a home, because night traffic varies by line.

Are there railroad quiet zones in Oklahoma City?

6 public crossings are reported in an FRA quiet zone. Status is reported per crossing, so confirm the crossing nearest the address.

Which railroads operate in Oklahoma City?

By number of public crossings: Union Pacific (46), Stillwater Central Railroad (35), BNSF (35), Oklahoma Railway Museum (5).

How fast do trains go through Oklahoma City?

The highest maximum timetable speed reported at these crossings is 79 mph. Many urban crossings report much lower limits; the busiest-crossing list shows the speed for each.

How hot does it get in Oklahoma City?

Oklahoma City averages about 73 days a year at 90°F or hotter and 13 at 100°F or hotter, with summer highs near 91°F (NOAA 1991–2020 normals).

Does it freeze or snow in Oklahoma City?

About 72 nights a year drop below freezing, and normal snowfall is 7 inches a year. Winter nights average 29°F.

Sources and limits
