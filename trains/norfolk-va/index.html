Common questions about trains in Norfolk

Do trains run at night in Norfolk?

29 of 29 crossings with reported traffic list trains between 6 PM and 6 AM. Check the specific crossing near a home, because night traffic varies by line.

Are there railroad quiet zones in Norfolk?

None of the 46 public crossings in this set is reported in an FRA quiet zone, so routine horn sounding is the default at public crossings.

Which railroads operate in Norfolk?

By number of public crossings: Norfolk Southern (42), Virginia Port Authority (3), Norfolk & Portsmouth Belt Line Railroad (1).

How fast do trains go through Norfolk?

The highest maximum timetable speed reported at these crossings is 25 mph. Many urban crossings report much lower limits; the busiest-crossing list shows the speed for each.

How hot does it get in Norfolk?

Norfolk averages about 42 days a year at 90°F or hotter and 1 at 100°F or hotter, with summer highs near 87°F (NOAA 1991–2020 normals).

Does it freeze or snow in Norfolk?

About 33 nights a year drop below freezing, and normal snowfall is 3 inches a year. Winter nights average 36°F.
