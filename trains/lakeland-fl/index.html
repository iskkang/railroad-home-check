What this means for a home search in Lakeland

Confirm the crossing, not the neighborhood. Quiet-zone status is reported crossing by crossing (6 of 60 here). Engineers can still sound horns for emergencies, and a home can sit between a quiet-zone crossing and one that is not. How quiet zones work.

Crossing incidents. FRA accident reports list 15 highway-rail incidents at 11 of these crossings between January 2021 and August 2026. That is a safety record for vehicles and pedestrians, not a noise measure. How to read crossing accident history.

Common questions about trains in Lakeland

Do trains run at night in Lakeland?

27 of 27 crossings with reported traffic list trains between 6 PM and 6 AM. Check the specific crossing near a home, because night traffic varies by line.

Are there railroad quiet zones in Lakeland?

6 public crossings are reported in an FRA quiet zone. Status is reported per crossing, so confirm the crossing nearest the address.

Which railroads operate in Lakeland?

By number of public crossings: CSX (60).

How fast do trains go through Lakeland?

The highest maximum timetable speed reported at these crossings is 79 mph. Many urban crossings report much lower limits; the busiest-crossing list shows the speed for each.

Is Lakeland a noisy place to live?

About 3% of Lakeland residents live where modeled transportation noise averages 55 dB or more over 24 hours (USDOT 2020 noise map), about the same as the median city among the 299 we cover. Roads are the biggest single source. Noise varies block by block, so check the specific address.

How hot does it get in Lakeland?

Lakeland averages about 121 days a year at 90°F or hotter and 0 at 100°F or hotter, with summer highs near 93°F (NOAA 1991–2020 normals).

Does it freeze or snow in Lakeland?

About 1 nights a year drop below freezing. Winter nights average 54°F.

Sources and limits

Incident counts come from FRA highway-rail accident reports (Form 57), January 2021–August 2026.
