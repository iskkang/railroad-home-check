FRA CROSSING DATA · TX

How Many Trains Run Through Dallas, TX?

The FRA crossing inventory lists 217 open public at-grade crossings in or near Dallas. 159 report at least one through train per day and 156 report trains between 6 PM and 6 AM. On railroad-company lines (excluding transit agencies), a typical active crossing reports about 4 trains per day; the busiest reports 35.

What this means for a home search in Dallas

Night trains are the norm, not the exception. 98% of Dallas crossings with reported traffic also report nighttime movements. If the home you are considering sits near any active line, plan at least one visit after dark.

Quiet zones are patchy. Only 5 of 217 public crossings (2%) are reported in an FRA quiet zone. Two homes a few blocks apart can have very different horn exposure, so check the crossing closest to each address. How quiet zones work.

The highest counts here come from passenger rail. 105 of the 217 crossings are on transit or commuter lines such as DART, Trinity Railway Express. Frequent, shorter trains produce the highest reported counts (up to 239 per day). Railroad-company lines are summarized separately below so the two do not get mixed up.

Many counts are old. 104 of 159 active crossings carry a train-count year before 2021 or no year at all (reported years range from 2000 to 2026). Treat older numbers as a rough guide and ask the seller or neighbors about current traffic.

Crossing incidents. FRA highway-rail accident reports list 40 incidents at 20 of these crossings between January 2021 and August 2026. That is a safety record for vehicles and pedestrians at the crossing, not a noise measure. How to read crossing accident history.

How to check a home near the tracks in Dallas

  1. Look up the address and find the nearest public crossing, not just the nearest track.
  2. Note the day and night counts, quiet-zone status and count year for that crossing ID.
  3. Visit once in daytime and once after 10 PM; stand outside and inside with windows closed.
  4. Ask the seller or neighbors whether traffic has changed since the count year.

Common questions about trains in Dallas

Do trains run at night in Dallas?

Yes on most active lines: 156 of 159 crossings with reported traffic list trains between 6 PM and 6 AM. Check the specific crossing near a home, because night traffic varies by line.

Are there railroad quiet zones in Dallas?

5 public crossings are reported in an FRA quiet zone. Status is reported per crossing, so confirm the crossing nearest the address.

Which railroads operate in Dallas?

By number of public crossings: DART (92), Dallas, Garland & Northeastern Railroad (38), Union Pacific (29), BNSF (25).

How fast do trains go through Dallas?

The highest maximum timetable speed reported at these crossings is 79 mph. Many urban crossings report much lower limits; the table above lists the speed for each busy crossing.

Sources and limits

Crossing counts come from the FRA Crossing Inventory (Form 71, current), filtered to open, public, at-grade crossings whose inventory city is Dallas (records reported as in or near the city). Incident counts come from FRA highway-rail accident reports (Form 57), January 2021–August 2026. Train counts are railroad-reported estimates with the listed count year; they are not live schedules, noise levels or safety ratings. Some records omit a count year or use placeholder values.
