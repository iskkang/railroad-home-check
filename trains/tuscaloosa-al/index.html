Common questions about trains in Tuscaloosa

Do trains run at night in Tuscaloosa?

Are there railroad quiet zones in Tuscaloosa?

None of the 67 public crossings in this set is reported in an FRA quiet zone, so routine horn sounding is the default at public crossings.

Which railroads operate in Tuscaloosa?

How fast do trains go through Tuscaloosa?

The highest maximum timetable speed reported at these crossings is 79 mph. Many urban crossings report much lower limits; the busiest-crossing list shows the speed for each.
