What this means for a home search in Bakersfield

Confirm the crossing, not the neighborhood. Quiet-zone status is reported crossing by crossing (7 of 68 here). Engineers can still sound horns for emergencies, and a home can sit between a quiet-zone crossing and one that is not. How quiet zones work.

Common questions about trains in Bakersfield

Do trains run at night in Bakersfield?

66 of 66 crossings with reported traffic list trains between 6 PM and 6 AM. Check the specific crossing near a home, because night traffic varies by line.

Are there railroad quiet zones in Bakersfield?

7 public crossings are reported in an FRA quiet zone. Status is reported per crossing, so confirm the crossing nearest the address.

Which railroads operate in Bakersfield?

By number of public crossings: San Joaquin Valley Railroad (53), BNSF (7), Union Pacific (7), Tulare Valley Railroad (1).

How fast do trains go through Bakersfield?

The highest maximum timetable speed reported at these crossings is 79 mph. Many urban crossings report much lower limits; the busiest-crossing list shows the speed for each.

Is Bakersfield a noisy place to live?

About 1% of Bakersfield residents live where modeled transportation noise averages 55 dB or more over 24 hours (USDOT 2020 noise map), less than the median city among the 299 we cover. Roads are the biggest single source. Noise varies block by block, so check the specific address.

How hot does it get in Bakersfield?

Bakersfield averages about 112 days a year at 90°F or hotter and 36 at 100°F or hotter, with summer highs near 96°F (NOAA 1991–2020 normals).

Does it freeze or snow in Bakersfield?

About 10 nights a year drop below freezing, and normal snowfall is 0 inches a year. Winter nights average 41°F.
