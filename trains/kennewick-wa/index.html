Common questions about trains in Kennewick

Do trains run at night in Kennewick?

Are there railroad quiet zones in Kennewick?

None of the 51 public crossings in this set is reported in an FRA quiet zone, so routine horn sounding is the default at public crossings.

How fast do trains go through Kennewick?

Sources and limits

Crossing counts come from the FRA Crossing Inventory (Form 71, current), filtered to open, public, at-grade crossings whose inventory city is Kennewick (records reported as in or near the city).
