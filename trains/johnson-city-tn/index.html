Common questions about trains in Johnson City

Do trains run at night in Johnson City?

24 of 35 crossings with reported traffic list trains between 6 PM and 6 AM. Check the specific crossing near a home, because night traffic varies by line.

Are there railroad quiet zones in Johnson City?

None of the 36 public crossings in this set is reported in an FRA quiet zone, so routine horn sounding is the default at public crossings.

Which railroads operate in Johnson City?

By number of public crossings: Norfolk Southern (21), East Tennessee Railway, L.P. (12), CSX (3).

How fast do trains go through Johnson City?

The highest maximum timetable speed reported at these crossings is 60 mph. Many urban crossings report much lower limits; the busiest-crossing list shows the speed for each.

How hot does it get in Johnson City?

Johnson City averages about 23 days a year at 90°F or hotter and 0 at 100°F or hotter, with summer highs near 85°F (NOAA 1991–2020 normals).

Does it freeze or snow in Johnson City?

About 94 nights a year drop below freezing, and normal snowfall is 9 inches a year. Winter nights average 28°F.
