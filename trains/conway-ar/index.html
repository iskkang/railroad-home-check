Common questions about trains in Conway

Do trains run at night in Conway?

16 of 16 crossings with reported traffic list trains between 6 PM and 6 AM. Check the specific crossing near a home, because night traffic varies by line.

Are there railroad quiet zones in Conway?

None of the 20 public crossings in this set is reported in an FRA quiet zone, so routine horn sounding is the default at public crossings.

Which railroads operate in Conway?

By number of public crossings: Union Pacific (20).

How fast do trains go through Conway?

The highest maximum timetable speed reported at these crossings is 50 mph. Many urban crossings report much lower limits; the busiest-crossing list shows the speed for each.

How hot does it get in Conway?

Conway averages about 70 days a year at 90°F or hotter and 6 at 100°F or hotter, with summer highs near 90°F (NOAA 1991–2020 normals).

Does it freeze or snow in Conway?

About 66 nights a year drop below freezing, and normal snowfall is 2 inches a year. Winter nights average 29°F.
