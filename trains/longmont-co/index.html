Common questions about trains in Longmont

Do trains run at night in Longmont?

20 of 23 crossings with reported traffic list trains between 6 PM and 6 AM. Check the specific crossing near a home, because night traffic varies by line.

Are there railroad quiet zones in Longmont?

None of the 36 public crossings in this set is reported in an FRA quiet zone, so routine horn sounding is the default at public crossings.

Which railroads operate in Longmont?

By number of public crossings: BNSF (31), Great Western Railway of Colorado (5).

How fast do trains go through Longmont?

The highest maximum timetable speed reported at these crossings is 49 mph. Many urban crossings report much lower limits; the busiest-crossing list shows the speed for each.

Is Longmont a noisy place to live?

About 1% of Longmont residents live where modeled transportation noise averages 55 dB or more over 24 hours (USDOT 2020 noise map), less than the median city among the 299 we cover. Roads are the biggest single source. Noise varies block by block, so check the specific address.

How hot does it get in Longmont?

Longmont averages about 49 days a year at 90°F or hotter and 4 at 100°F or hotter, with summer highs near 88°F (NOAA 1991–2020 normals).

Does it freeze or snow in Longmont?

About 178 nights a year drop below freezing. Winter nights average 15°F.
