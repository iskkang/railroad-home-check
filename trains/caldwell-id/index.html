What this means for a home search in Caldwell

Confirm the crossing, not the neighborhood. Quiet-zone status is reported crossing by crossing (7 of 33 here). Engineers can still sound horns for emergencies, and a home can sit between a quiet-zone crossing and one that is not. How quiet zones work.

Common questions about trains in Caldwell

Do trains run at night in Caldwell?

17 of 29 crossings with reported traffic list trains between 6 PM and 6 AM. Check the specific crossing near a home, because night traffic varies by line.

Are there railroad quiet zones in Caldwell?

7 public crossings are reported in an FRA quiet zone. Status is reported per crossing, so confirm the crossing nearest the address.

Which railroads operate in Caldwell?

By number of public crossings: Union Pacific (17), Boise Valley Railroad (16).

How fast do trains go through Caldwell?

The highest maximum timetable speed reported at these crossings is 70 mph. Many urban crossings report much lower limits; the busiest-crossing list shows the speed for each.

How hot does it get in Caldwell?

Caldwell averages about 59 days a year at 90°F or hotter and 10 at 100°F or hotter, with summer highs near 89°F (NOAA 1991–2020 normals).

Does it freeze or snow in Caldwell?

About 132 nights a year drop below freezing, and normal snowfall is 10 inches a year. Winter nights average 24°F.
