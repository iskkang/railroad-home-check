Common questions about trains in Bowling Green

Do trains run at night in Bowling Green?

17 of 20 crossings with reported traffic list trains between 6 PM and 6 AM. Check the specific crossing near a home, because night traffic varies by line.

Are there railroad quiet zones in Bowling Green?

None of the 23 public crossings in this set is reported in an FRA quiet zone, so routine horn sounding is the default at public crossings.

Which railroads operate in Bowling Green?

By number of public crossings: CSX (20), R. J. Corman Railroad Company/Memphis Line (3).

How fast do trains go through Bowling Green?

The highest maximum timetable speed reported at these crossings is 60 mph. Many urban crossings report much lower limits; the busiest-crossing list shows the speed for each.

Is Bowling Green a noisy place to live?

About 6% of Bowling Green residents live where modeled transportation noise averages 55 dB or more over 24 hours (USDOT 2020 noise map), more than the median city among the 299 we cover. Roads are the biggest single source. Noise varies block by block, so check the specific address.

How hot does it get in Bowling Green?

Bowling Green averages about 47 days a year at 90°F or hotter and 1 at 100°F or hotter, with summer highs near 89°F (NOAA 1991–2020 normals).

Does it freeze or snow in Bowling Green?

About 82 nights a year drop below freezing, and normal snowfall is 9 inches a year. Winter nights average 30°F.
