What this means for a home search in Folsom

Horns are the default. None of the 31 public crossings is reported in an FRA quiet zone, so routine horn sounding at public crossings applies under the federal Train Horn Rule. Distance to the nearest crossing matters more here than in cities with broad quiet-zone coverage. Train horns near a house.

The highest counts come from passenger rail. 11 of the 31 crossings are on transit or commuter lines such as SacRT, with counts up to 76 trains a day. Railroad-company lines report little traffic here, so the busiest-crossing list includes every operator.

Many counts are old. 12 of 15 active crossings carry a count year before 2021 or none at all (range 2017–2021). Ask the seller or neighbors whether traffic has changed.

Crossing incidents. FRA accident reports list 0 highway-rail incidents at 0 of these crossings between January 2021 and August 2026. That is a safety record for vehicles and pedestrians, not a noise measure. How to read crossing accident history.

Do trains run at night in Folsom?

Is Folsom a noisy place to live?
