Common questions about trains in Indianapolis

Do trains run at night in Indianapolis?

60 of 71 crossings with reported traffic list trains between 6 PM and 6 AM. Check the specific crossing near a home, because night traffic varies by line.

Are there railroad quiet zones in Indianapolis?

None of the 168 public crossings in this set is reported in an FRA quiet zone, so routine horn sounding is the default at public crossings.

Which railroads operate in Indianapolis?

By number of public crossings: CSX (141), Indiana Rail Road (14), Louisville & Indiana Railroad (8), Indiana Southern Railroad (5).

How fast do trains go through Indianapolis?

The highest maximum timetable speed reported at these crossings is 60 mph. Many urban crossings report much lower limits; the busiest-crossing list shows the speed for each.

Is Indianapolis a noisy place to live?

About 4% of Indianapolis residents live where modeled transportation noise averages 55 dB or more over 24 hours (USDOT 2020 noise map), more than the median city among the 299 we cover. Roads are the biggest single source. Noise varies block by block, so check the specific address.

How hot does it get in Indianapolis?

Indianapolis averages about 20 days a year at 90°F or hotter and 0 at 100°F or hotter, with summer highs near 84°F (NOAA 1991–2020 normals).

Does it freeze or snow in Indianapolis?

About 106 nights a year drop below freezing, and normal snowfall is 26 inches a year. Winter nights average 24°F.

Sources and limits
