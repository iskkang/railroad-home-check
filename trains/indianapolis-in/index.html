How Many Trains Run Through Indianapolis, IN?

The FRA crossing inventory lists 168 open public at-grade crossings in or near Indianapolis. 71 report at least one through train per day and 60 report trains between 6 PM and 6 AM. On railroad-company lines (excluding transit agencies), a typical active crossing reports about 4 trains per day; the busiest reports 8.

Data retrieved November 9, 2026 from the FRA crossing inventory (latest record update 2026-11-06).

What this means for a home search in Indianapolis

Night trains are the norm, not the exception. 85% of Indianapolis crossings with reported traffic also report nighttime movements. If the home you are considering sits near any active line, plan at least one visit after dark.

No quiet zones appear in this set. None of the 168 public crossings is reported in an FRA quiet zone, so routine horn sounding at public crossings is the default under the federal Train Horn Rule. Distance to the nearest crossing matters more here than in cities with broad quiet-zone coverage.

Most counts are recent. 47 of 71 active crossings report a train-count year of 2021 or later (range 2016–2026). Counts are still reported estimates, not schedules.

Crossing incidents. FRA highway-rail accident reports list 32 incidents at 24 of these crossings between January 2021 and August 2026. That is a safety record for vehicles and pedestrians at the crossing, not a noise measure. How to read crossing accident history.

The busiest railroad-company crossing in this set is East Edgewood Ave (Louisville & Indiana Railroad), reporting 6 day and 2 night through trains as of 2016. How to check a home near the tracks in Indianapolis

  1. Look up the address and find the nearest public crossing, not just the nearest track.
  2. Note the day and night counts, quiet-zone status and count year for that crossing ID.
  3. Visit once in daytime and once after 10 PM; stand outside and inside with windows closed.
  4. Ask the seller or neighbors whether traffic has changed since the count year.

Common questions about trains in Indianapolis

Do trains run at night in Indianapolis?

Yes on most active lines: 60 of 71 crossings with reported traffic list trains between 6 PM and 6 AM. Check the specific crossing near a home, because night traffic varies by line.

Are there railroad quiet zones in Indianapolis?

None of the 168 public crossings in this set is reported in an FRA quiet zone, so routine horn sounding is the default at public crossings.

Which railroads operate in Indianapolis?

By number of public crossings: CSX (141), Indiana Rail Road (14), Louisville & Indiana Railroad (8), Indiana Southern Railroad (5).

How fast do trains go through Indianapolis?

The highest maximum timetable speed reported at these crossings is 60 mph. Many urban crossings report much lower limits; the table above lists the speed for each busy crossing.

Sources and limits

Crossing counts come from the FRA Crossing Inventory (Form 71, current), filtered to open, public, at-grade crossings whose inventory city is Indianapolis (records reported as in or near the city). Incident counts come from FRA highway-rail accident reports (Form 57), January 2021–August 2026. Train counts are railroad-reported estimates with the listed count year; they are not live schedules, noise levels or safety ratings. Some records omit a count year or use placeholder values.
