Common questions about trains in Gulfport

Do trains run at night in Gulfport?

49 of 49 crossings with reported traffic list trains between 6 PM and 6 AM. Check the specific crossing near a home, because night traffic varies by line.

Are there railroad quiet zones in Gulfport?

None of the 67 public crossings in this set is reported in an FRA quiet zone, so routine horn sounding is the default at public crossings.

Which railroads operate in Gulfport?

By number of public crossings: Kansas City Southern (CPKC) (31), CSX (26), None (10).

How fast do trains go through Gulfport?

The highest maximum timetable speed reported at these crossings is 60 mph. Many urban crossings report much lower limits; the busiest-crossing list shows the speed for each.

How hot does it get in Gulfport?

Gulfport averages about 69 days a year at 90°F or hotter and 1 at 100°F or hotter, with summer highs near 90°F (NOAA 1991–2020 normals).

Does it freeze or snow in Gulfport?

About 16 nights a year drop below freezing. Winter nights average 44°F.
