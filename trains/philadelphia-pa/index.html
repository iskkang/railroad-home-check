What this means for a home search in Philadelphia

Horns are the default. None of the 108 public crossings is reported in an FRA quiet zone, so routine horn sounding at public crossings applies under the federal Train Horn Rule. Distance to the nearest crossing matters more here than in cities with broad quiet-zone coverage. Train horns near a house.

The highest counts come from passenger rail. 19 of the 108 crossings are on transit or commuter lines such as SEPTA, with counts up to 78 trains a day. Railroad-company lines are listed separately below.

Crossing incidents. FRA accident reports list 9 highway-rail incidents at 7 of these crossings between January 2021 and August 2026. That is a safety record for vehicles and pedestrians, not a noise measure. How to read crossing accident history.

Common questions about trains in Philadelphia

Do trains run at night in Philadelphia?

28 of 37 crossings with reported traffic list trains between 6 PM and 6 AM. Check the specific crossing near a home, because night traffic varies by line.

Are there railroad quiet zones in Philadelphia?

None of the 108 public crossings in this set is reported in an FRA quiet zone, so routine horn sounding is the default at public crossings.

Which railroads operate in Philadelphia?

By number of public crossings: Conrail Shared Assets (72), SEPTA (19), CSX (9), Philadelphia Belt Line Railroad (7).

How fast do trains go through Philadelphia?

The highest maximum timetable speed reported at these crossings is 80 mph. Many urban crossings report much lower limits; the busiest-crossing list shows the speed for each.

Sources and limits

Crossing counts come from the FRA Crossing Inventory (Form 71, current), filtered to open, public, at-grade crossings whose inventory city is Philadelphia (records reported as in or near the city). Incident counts come from FRA highway-rail accident reports (Form 57), January 2021–August 2026. Train counts are railroad-reported estimates with the listed count year; they are not live schedules, noise levels or safety ratings. Read our source coverage and limitations and corrections policy.
