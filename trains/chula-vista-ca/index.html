What this means for a home search in Chula Vista

Horns are the default. None of the 21 public crossings is reported in an FRA quiet zone, so routine horn sounding at public crossings applies under the federal Train Horn Rule. Distance to the nearest crossing matters more here than in cities with broad quiet-zone coverage. Train horns near a house.

The highest counts come from passenger rail. 14 of the 21 crossings are on transit or commuter lines such as San Diego Trolley, with counts up to 209 trains a day. Railroad-company lines report little traffic here, so the busiest-crossing list includes every operator.

Many counts are old. 14 of 14 active crossings carry a count year before 2021 or none at all (range 2014–2019). Ask the seller or neighbors whether traffic has changed.

Crossing incidents. FRA accident reports list 5 highway-rail incidents at 3 of these crossings between January 2021 and August 2026. That is a safety record for vehicles and pedestrians, not a noise measure. How to read crossing accident history.
