FRA CROSSING DATA · MO

How Many Trains Run Through St. Louis, MO?

The FRA crossing inventory lists 96 open public at-grade crossings in or near St. Louis. 69 report at least one through train per day and 62 report trains between 6 PM and 6 AM. On railroad-company lines (excluding transit agencies), a typical active crossing reports about 6 trains per day; the busiest reports 20.

Data retrieved November 9, 2026 from the FRA crossing inventory (latest record update 2026-11-02).

What this means for a home search in St. Louis

Night trains are the norm, not the exception. 90% of St. Louis crossings with reported traffic also report nighttime movements. If the home you are considering sits near any active line, plan at least one visit after dark.

Quiet zones cover much of the network. 92 of 96 public crossings (96%) are reported in 24-hour quiet zones. Engineers can still sound horns for emergencies and some crossings sit just outside a zone, so confirm the status of the specific crossing nearest the home. How quiet zones work.

Many counts are old. 51 of 69 active crossings carry a train-count year before 2021 or no year at all (reported years range from 2016 to 2025). Treat older numbers as a rough guide and ask the seller or neighbors about current traffic.

Crossing incidents. FRA highway-rail accident reports list 2 incidents at 2 of these crossings between January 2021 and August 2026. That is a safety record for vehicles and pedestrians at the crossing, not a noise measure. How to read crossing accident history.

How to check a home near the tracks in St. Louis

  1. Look up the address and find the nearest public crossing, not just the nearest track.
  2. Note the day and night counts, quiet-zone status and count year for that crossing ID.
  3. Visit once in daytime and once after 10 PM; stand outside and inside with windows closed.
  4. Ask the seller or neighbors whether traffic has changed since the count year.

Common questions about trains in St. Louis

Do trains run at night in St. Louis?

Yes on most active lines: 62 of 69 crossings with reported traffic list trains between 6 PM and 6 AM. Check the specific crossing near a home, because night traffic varies by line.

Are there railroad quiet zones in St. Louis?

92 public crossings are reported in an FRA quiet zone. Status is reported per crossing, so confirm the crossing nearest the address.

Which railroads operate in St. Louis?

By number of public crossings: Terminal Railroad Association of St. Louis (36), Manufacturers' Railway (19), Norfolk Southern (19), BNSF (11).

How fast do trains go through St. Louis?

The highest maximum timetable speed reported at these crossings is 50 mph. Many urban crossings report much lower limits; the table above lists the speed for each busy crossing.

Sources and limits

Crossing counts come from the FRA Crossing Inventory (Form 71, current), filtered to open, public, at-grade crossings whose inventory city is St. Louis (records reported as in or near the city). Incident counts come from FRA highway-rail accident reports (Form 57), January 2021–August 2026. Train counts are railroad-reported estimates with the listed count year; they are not live schedules, noise levels or safety ratings. Some records omit a count year or use placeholder values.
