What this means for a home search in Evansville

Horns are the default. None of the 99 public crossings is reported in an FRA quiet zone, so routine horn sounding at public crossings applies under the federal Train Horn Rule. Common questions about trains in Evansville

Do trains run at night in Evansville?

35 of 38 crossings with reported traffic list trains between 6 PM and 6 AM. Check the specific crossing near a home, because night traffic varies by line.

Are there railroad quiet zones in Evansville?

None of the 99 public crossings in this set is reported in an FRA quiet zone, so routine horn sounding is the default at public crossings.

Which railroads operate in Evansville?

By number of public crossings: CSX (63), Norfolk Southern (15), Evansville Western Railway (10), Indiana Southern Railroad (10).

How fast do trains go through Evansville?

The highest maximum timetable speed reported at these crossings is 60 mph. Many urban crossings report much lower limits; the busiest-crossing list shows the speed for each.

Is Evansville a noisy place to live?

About 3% of Evansville residents live where modeled transportation noise averages 55 dB or more over 24 hours (USDOT 2020 noise map), about the same as the median city among the 299 we cover. Roads are the biggest single source. Noise varies block by block, so check the specific address.

How hot does it get in Evansville?

Evansville averages about 41 days a year at 90°F or hotter and 1 at 100°F or hotter, with summer highs near 87°F (NOAA 1991–2020 normals).

Does it freeze or snow in Evansville?

About 90 nights a year drop below freezing, and normal snowfall is 11 inches a year. Winter nights average 28°F.
