Common questions about trains in Macon

Do trains run at night in Macon?

27 of 37 crossings with reported traffic list trains between 6 PM and 6 AM. Check the specific crossing near a home, because night traffic varies by line.

Are there railroad quiet zones in Macon?

None of the 64 public crossings in this set is reported in an FRA quiet zone, so routine horn sounding is the default at public crossings.

Which railroads operate in Macon?

By number of public crossings: Norfolk Southern (49), Georgia Central Railway, L.P. (15).

How fast do trains go through Macon?

The highest maximum timetable speed reported at these crossings is 60 mph. Many urban crossings report much lower limits; the busiest-crossing list shows the speed for each.

Is Macon a noisy place to live?

About 3% of Macon residents live where modeled transportation noise averages 55 dB or more over 24 hours (USDOT 2020 noise map), about the same as the median city among the 299 we cover. Roads are the biggest single source. Noise varies block by block, so check the specific address.

How hot does it get in Macon?

Macon averages about 85 days a year at 90°F or hotter and 4 at 100°F or hotter, with summer highs near 92°F (NOAA 1991–2020 normals).

Does it freeze or snow in Macon?

About 44 nights a year drop below freezing. Winter nights average 38°F.
