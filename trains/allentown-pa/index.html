What this means for a home search in Allentown

Horns are the default. None of the 17 public crossings is reported in an FRA quiet zone, so routine horn sounding at public crossings applies under the federal Train Horn Rule. Distance to the nearest crossing matters more here than in cities with broad quiet-zone coverage. Train horns near a house.

Common questions about trains in Allentown

Do trains run at night in Allentown?

6 of 11 crossings with reported traffic list trains between 6 PM and 6 AM. Check the specific crossing near a home, because night traffic varies by line.

Are there railroad quiet zones in Allentown?

None of the 17 public crossings in this set is reported in an FRA quiet zone, so routine horn sounding is the default at public crossings.

Which railroads operate in Allentown?

By number of public crossings: Norfolk Southern (15), R. J. Corman Railroad Co./Allentown Lines (2).

How fast do trains go through Allentown?

The highest maximum timetable speed reported at these crossings is 150 mph. Many urban crossings report much lower limits; the busiest-crossing list shows the speed for each.

Is Allentown a noisy place to live?

About 4% of Allentown residents live where modeled transportation noise averages 55 dB or more over 24 hours (USDOT 2020 noise map), more than the median city among the 299 we cover. Roads are the biggest single source. Noise varies block by block, so check the specific address.

How hot does it get in Allentown?

Allentown averages about 18 days a year at 90°F or hotter and 0 at 100°F or hotter, with summer highs near 84°F (NOAA 1991–2020 normals).

Does it freeze or snow in Allentown?

About 118 nights a year drop below freezing, and normal snowfall is 33 inches a year. Winter nights average 24°F.
