Common questions about trains in Antioch

Do trains run at night in Antioch?

11 of 11 crossings with reported traffic list trains between 6 PM and 6 AM. Check the specific crossing near a home, because night traffic varies by line.

Are there railroad quiet zones in Antioch?

None of the 15 public crossings in this set is reported in an FRA quiet zone, so routine horn sounding is the default at public crossings.

Which railroads operate in Antioch?

By number of public crossings: BNSF (11), Union Pacific (4).

How fast do trains go through Antioch?

The highest maximum timetable speed reported at these crossings is 79 mph. Many urban crossings report much lower limits; the busiest-crossing list shows the speed for each.

Is Antioch a noisy place to live?

About 0% of Antioch residents live where modeled transportation noise averages 55 dB or more over 24 hours (USDOT 2020 noise map), less than the median city among the 299 we cover. Noise varies block by block, so check the specific address.

How hot does it get in Antioch?

Antioch averages about 51 days a year at 90°F or hotter and 9 at 100°F or hotter, with summer highs near 87°F (NOAA 1991–2020 normals).

Does it freeze or snow in Antioch?

About 9 nights a year drop below freezing. Winter nights average 42°F.
