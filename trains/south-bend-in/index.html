What this means for a home search in South Bend

Confirm the crossing, not the neighborhood. Quiet-zone status is reported crossing by crossing (17 of 44 here). Engineers can still sound horns for emergencies, and a home can sit between a quiet-zone crossing and one that is not. How quiet zones work.

The highest counts come from passenger rail. 14 of the 44 crossings are on transit or commuter lines such as South Shore Line (NICTD), with counts up to 63 trains a day. Railroad-company lines are listed separately below.

Crossing incidents. FRA accident reports list 17 highway-rail incidents at 9 of these crossings between January 2021 and August 2026. That is a safety record for vehicles and pedestrians, not a noise measure. How to read crossing accident history.

Common questions about trains in South Bend

Do trains run at night in South Bend?

38 of 40 crossings with reported traffic list trains between 6 PM and 6 AM. Check the specific crossing near a home, because night traffic varies by line.

Are there railroad quiet zones in South Bend?

17 public crossings are reported in an FRA quiet zone. Status is reported per crossing, so confirm the crossing nearest the address.

Which railroads operate in South Bend?

By number of public crossings: Grand Trunk Western (CN) (15), South Shore Line (NICTD) (14), Norfolk Southern (12), Chicago Southshore & South Bend Railroad (3).

How fast do trains go through South Bend?

The highest maximum timetable speed reported at these crossings is 79 mph. Many urban crossings report much lower limits; the busiest-crossing list shows the speed for each.

Sources and limits

Crossing counts come from the FRA Crossing Inventory (Form 71, current), filtered to open, public, at-grade crossings whose inventory city is South Bend (records reported as in or near the city). Incident counts come from FRA highway-rail accident reports (Form 57), January 2021–August 2026. Train counts are railroad-reported estimates with the listed count year; they are not live schedules, noise levels or safety ratings. Read our source coverage and limitations and corrections policy.
