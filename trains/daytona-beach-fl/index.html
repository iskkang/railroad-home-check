Common questions about trains in Daytona Beach

Do trains run at night in Daytona Beach?

15 of 15 crossings with reported traffic list trains between 6 PM and 6 AM. Check the specific crossing near a home, because night traffic varies by line.

Are there railroad quiet zones in Daytona Beach?

None of the 15 public crossings in this set is reported in an FRA quiet zone, so routine horn sounding is the default at public crossings.

Which railroads operate in Daytona Beach?

By number of public crossings: Florida East Coast Railway (15).

How fast do trains go through Daytona Beach?

The highest maximum timetable speed reported at these crossings is 45 mph. Many urban crossings report much lower limits; the busiest-crossing list shows the speed for each.

How hot does it get in Daytona Beach?

Daytona Beach averages about 65 days a year at 90°F or hotter and 0 at 100°F or hotter, with summer highs near 90°F (NOAA 1991–2020 normals).

Does it freeze or snow in Daytona Beach?

About 3 nights a year drop below freezing. Winter nights average 51°F.
