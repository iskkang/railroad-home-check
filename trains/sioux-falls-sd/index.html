Common questions about trains in Sioux Falls

Do trains run at night in Sioux Falls?

14 of 38 crossings with reported traffic list trains between 6 PM and 6 AM. Check the specific crossing near a home, because night traffic varies by line.

Are there railroad quiet zones in Sioux Falls?

None of the 92 public crossings in this set is reported in an FRA quiet zone, so routine horn sounding is the default at public crossings.

Which railroads operate in Sioux Falls?

By number of public crossings: BNSF (55), Ellis and Eastern (36), D & I Railroad (1).

How fast do trains go through Sioux Falls?

The highest maximum timetable speed reported at these crossings is 40 mph. Many urban crossings report much lower limits; the busiest-crossing list shows the speed for each.

Is Sioux Falls a noisy place to live?

About 8% of Sioux Falls residents live where modeled transportation noise averages 55 dB or more over 24 hours (USDOT 2020 noise map), more than the median city among the 299 we cover. Roads are the biggest single source. Noise varies block by block, so check the specific address.

How hot does it get in Sioux Falls?

Sioux Falls averages about 15 days a year at 90°F or hotter and 1 at 100°F or hotter, with summer highs near 83°F (NOAA 1991–2020 normals).

Does it freeze or snow in Sioux Falls?

About 162 nights a year drop below freezing, and normal snowfall is 45 inches a year. Winter nights average 12°F.
