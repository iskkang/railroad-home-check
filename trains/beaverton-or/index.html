What this means for a home search in Beaverton

Horns are the default. None of the 35 public crossings is reported in an FRA quiet zone, so routine horn sounding at public crossings applies under the federal Train Horn Rule. Common questions about trains in Beaverton

Do trains run at night in Beaverton?

31 of 31 crossings with reported traffic list trains between 6 PM and 6 AM. Check the specific crossing near a home, because night traffic varies by line.

Are there railroad quiet zones in Beaverton?

None of the 35 public crossings in this set is reported in an FRA quiet zone, so routine horn sounding is the default at public crossings.

Which railroads operate in Beaverton?

By number of public crossings: Portland & Western Railroad (22), TriMet MAX (13).

How fast do trains go through Beaverton?

The highest maximum timetable speed reported at these crossings is 60 mph. Many urban crossings report much lower limits; the busiest-crossing list shows the speed for each.

Is Beaverton a noisy place to live?

About 6% of Beaverton residents live where modeled transportation noise averages 55 dB or more over 24 hours (USDOT 2020 noise map), more than the median city among the 299 we cover. Noise varies block by block, so check the specific address.

How hot does it get in Beaverton?

Beaverton averages about 17 days a year at 90°F or hotter and 1 at 100°F or hotter, with summer highs near 79°F (NOAA 1991–2020 normals).

Does it freeze or snow in Beaverton?

About 64 nights a year drop below freezing. Winter nights average 34°F.
