FRA CROSSING DATA · IL

How Many Trains Run Through Chicago, IL?

The FRA crossing inventory lists 303 open public at-grade crossings in or near Chicago. 258 report at least one through train per day and 241 report trains between 6 PM and 6 AM. On railroad-company lines (excluding transit agencies), a typical active crossing reports about 12 trains per day; the busiest reports 79.

Data retrieved November 9, 2026 from the FRA crossing inventory (latest record update 2026-11-06).

What this means for a home search in Chicago

Night trains are the norm, not the exception. 93% of Chicago crossings with reported traffic also report nighttime movements. If the home you are considering sits near any active line, plan at least one visit after dark.

Chicago has its own horn rules. 118 crossings are reported as Chicago-excused, 14 as 24-hour quiet zones and 1 as a partial quiet zone. Quiet-zone status limits routine horn sounding but does not silence emergency horns, bells or wayside equipment. Read the quiet-zone guide.

The highest counts here come from passenger rail. 151 of the 303 crossings are on transit or commuter lines such as Metra. Frequent, shorter trains produce the highest reported counts (up to 215 per day). Railroad-company lines are summarized separately below so the two do not get mixed up.

Many counts are old. 180 of 258 active crossings carry a train-count year before 2021 or no year at all (reported years range from 2014 to 2026). Treat older numbers as a rough guide and ask the seller or neighbors about current traffic.

Crossing incidents. FRA highway-rail accident reports list 102 incidents at 63 of these crossings between January 2021 and August 2026. That is a safety record for vehicles and pedestrians at the crossing, not a noise measure. How to read crossing accident history.

How to check a home near the tracks in Chicago

  1. Look up the address and find the nearest public crossing, not just the nearest track.
  2. Note the day and night counts, quiet-zone status and count year for that crossing ID.
  3. Visit once in daytime and once after 10 PM; stand outside and inside with windows closed.
  4. Ask the seller or neighbors whether traffic has changed since the count year.

Common questions about trains in Chicago

Do trains run at night in Chicago?

Yes on most active lines: 241 of 258 crossings with reported traffic list trains between 6 PM and 6 AM. Check the specific crossing near a home, because night traffic varies by line.

Are there railroad quiet zones in Chicago?

133 public crossings are reported in an FRA quiet zone (including 118 Chicago-excused crossings). Status is reported per crossing, so confirm the crossing nearest the address.

Which railroads operate in Chicago?

By number of public crossings: Metra (138), Union Pacific (35), Norfolk Southern (33), Belt Railway of Chicago (25).

How fast do trains go through Chicago?

The highest maximum timetable speed reported at these crossings is 79 mph. Many urban crossings report much lower limits; the table above lists the speed for each busy crossing.

Sources and limits

Crossing counts come from the FRA Crossing Inventory (Form 71, current), filtered to open, public, at-grade crossings whose inventory city is Chicago (records reported as in or near the city). Incident counts come from FRA highway-rail accident reports (Form 57), January 2021–August 2026. Train counts are railroad-reported estimates with the listed count year; they are not live schedules, noise levels or safety ratings. Some records omit a count year or use placeholder values.
