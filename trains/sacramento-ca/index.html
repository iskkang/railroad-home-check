What this means for a home search in Sacramento

Confirm the crossing, not the neighborhood. Quiet-zone status is reported crossing by crossing (33 of 82 here). Engineers can still sound horns for emergencies, and a home can sit between a quiet-zone crossing and one that is not. How quiet zones work.

The highest counts come from passenger rail. 21 of the 82 crossings are on transit or commuter lines such as SacRT, with counts up to 142 trains a day. Railroad-company lines are listed separately below.

Many counts are old. 22 of 54 active crossings carry a count year before 2021 or none at all (range 2015–2025). Ask the seller or neighbors whether traffic has changed.

Crossing incidents. FRA accident reports list 9 highway-rail incidents at 7 of these crossings between January 2021 and August 2026. That is a safety record for vehicles and pedestrians, not a noise measure. How to read crossing accident history.

Common questions about trains in Sacramento

Do trains run at night in Sacramento?

50 of 54 crossings with reported traffic list trains between 6 PM and 6 AM. Check the specific crossing near a home, because night traffic varies by line.

Are there railroad quiet zones in Sacramento?

33 public crossings are reported in an FRA quiet zone. Status is reported per crossing, so confirm the crossing nearest the address.

Which railroads operate in Sacramento?

By number of public crossings: Union Pacific (39), SacRT (21), Sacramento Valley Railroad (6), Central California Traction (6).

How fast do trains go through Sacramento?

The highest maximum timetable speed reported at these crossings is 79 mph. Many urban crossings report much lower limits; the busiest-crossing list shows the speed for each.

Is Sacramento a noisy place to live?

About 6% of Sacramento residents live where modeled transportation noise averages 55 dB or more over 24 hours (USDOT 2020 noise map), more than the median city among the 299 we cover. Roads are the biggest single source. Noise varies block by block, so check the specific address.

How hot does it get in Sacramento?

Sacramento averages about 90 days a year at 90°F or hotter and 23 at 100°F or hotter, with summer highs near 92°F (NOAA 1991–2020 normals).

Does it freeze or snow in Sacramento?

About 4 nights a year drop below freezing, and normal snowfall is 0 inches a year. Winter nights average 42°F.

Sources and limits

Crossing counts come from the FRA Crossing Inventory (Form 71, current), filtered to open, public, at-grade crossings whose inventory city is Sacramento (records reported as in or near the city). Incident counts come from FRA highway-rail accident reports (Form 57), January 2021–August 2026. Train counts are railroad-reported estimates with the listed count year; they are not live schedules, noise levels or safety ratings. Read our source coverage and limitations and corrections policy.
