What this means for a home search in Rogers

Horns are the default. None of the 30 public crossings is reported in an FRA quiet zone, so routine horn sounding at public crossings applies under the federal Train Horn Rule. Distance to the nearest crossing matters more here than in cities with broad quiet-zone coverage. Train horns near a house.

Many counts are old. 14 of 21 active crossings carry a count year before 2021 or none at all (range 2017–2026). Ask the seller or neighbors whether traffic has changed.

Crossing incidents. FRA accident reports list 2 highway-rail incidents at 2 of these crossings between January 2021 and August 2026. That is a safety record for vehicles and pedestrians, not a noise measure. How to read crossing accident history.

Common questions about trains in Rogers

Do trains run at night in Rogers?

4 of 21 crossings with reported traffic list trains between 6 PM and 6 AM. Check the specific crossing near a home, because night traffic varies by line.

Are there railroad quiet zones in Rogers?

None of the 30 public crossings in this set is reported in an FRA quiet zone, so routine horn sounding is the default at public crossings.

Which railroads operate in Rogers?

By number of public crossings: Arkansas & Missouri Railroad (30).

How fast do trains go through Rogers?

The highest maximum timetable speed reported at these crossings is 35 mph. Many urban crossings report much lower limits; the busiest-crossing list shows the speed for each.

How hot does it get in Rogers?

Rogers averages about 45 days a year at 90°F or hotter and 4 at 100°F or hotter, with summer highs near 89°F (NOAA 1991–2020 normals).

Does it freeze or snow in Rogers?

About 92 nights a year drop below freezing. Winter nights average 29°F.
