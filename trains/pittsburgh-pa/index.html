What this means for a home search in Pittsburgh

Common questions about trains in Pittsburgh

Do trains run at night in Pittsburgh?

18 of 19 crossings with reported traffic list trains between 6 PM and 6 AM. Check the specific crossing near a home, because night traffic varies by line.

Are there railroad quiet zones in Pittsburgh?

None of the 37 public crossings in this set is reported in an FRA quiet zone, so routine horn sounding is the default at public crossings.

Which railroads operate in Pittsburgh?

By number of public crossings: Allegheny Valley Railroad (29), CSX (4), Pittsburgh & Ohio Central Railroad Company, The (3), Norfolk Southern (1).

How fast do trains go through Pittsburgh?

The highest maximum timetable speed reported at these crossings is 40 mph. Many urban crossings report much lower limits; the busiest-crossing list shows the speed for each.

Sources and limits

Crossing counts come from the FRA Crossing Inventory (Form 71, current), filtered to open, public, at-grade crossings whose inventory city is Pittsburgh (records reported as in or near the city). Incident counts come from FRA highway-rail accident reports (Form 57), January 2021–August 2026. Train counts are railroad-reported estimates with the listed count year; they are not live schedules, noise levels or safety ratings. Read our source coverage and limitations and corrections policy.
