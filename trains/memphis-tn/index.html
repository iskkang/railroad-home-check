FRA CROSSING DATA · TN

How Many Trains Run Through Memphis, TN?

The FRA crossing inventory lists 160 open public at-grade crossings in or near Memphis. 109 report at least one through train per day and 105 report trains between 6 PM and 6 AM. On railroad-company lines (excluding transit agencies), a typical active crossing reports about 8 trains per day; the busiest reports 32.

What this means for a home search in Memphis

Night trains are the norm, not the exception. 96% of Memphis crossings with reported traffic also report nighttime movements. If the home you are considering sits near any active line, plan at least one visit after dark.

Quiet zones are patchy. Only 6 of 160 public crossings (4%) are reported in an FRA quiet zone. Two homes a few blocks apart can have very different horn exposure, so check the crossing closest to each address. How quiet zones work.

Most counts are recent. 103 of 109 active crossings report a train-count year of 2021 or later (range 2015–2026). Counts are still reported estimates, not schedules.

Crossing incidents. FRA highway-rail accident reports list 63 incidents at 34 of these crossings between January 2021 and August 2026. That is a safety record for vehicles and pedestrians at the crossing, not a noise measure. How to read crossing accident history.

How to check a home near the tracks in Memphis

  1. Look up the address and find the nearest public crossing, not just the nearest track.
  2. Note the day and night counts, quiet-zone status and count year for that crossing ID.
  3. Visit once in daytime and once after 10 PM; stand outside and inside with windows closed.
  4. Ask the seller or neighbors whether traffic has changed since the count year.

Common questions about trains in Memphis

Do trains run at night in Memphis?

Yes on most active lines: 105 of 109 crossings with reported traffic list trains between 6 PM and 6 AM. Check the specific crossing near a home, because night traffic varies by line.

Are there railroad quiet zones in Memphis?

6 public crossings are reported in an FRA quiet zone. Status is reported per crossing, so confirm the crossing nearest the address.

Which railroads operate in Memphis?

By number of public crossings: Illinois Central (CN) (54), R.J. Corman (25), Norfolk Southern (24), BNSF (22).

How fast do trains go through Memphis?

The highest maximum timetable speed reported at these crossings is 60 mph. Many urban crossings report much lower limits; the table above lists the speed for each busy crossing.

Sources and limits

Crossing counts come from the FRA Crossing Inventory (Form 71, current), filtered to open, public, at-grade crossings whose inventory city is Memphis (records reported as in or near the city). Incident counts come from FRA highway-rail accident reports (Form 57), January 2021–August 2026. Train counts are railroad-reported estimates with the listed count year; they are not live schedules, noise levels or safety ratings. Some records omit a count year or use placeholder values.
