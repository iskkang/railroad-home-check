What this means for a home search in Fresno

Confirm the crossing, not the neighborhood. Quiet-zone status is reported crossing by crossing (14 of 138 here). Engineers can still sound horns for emergencies, and a home can sit between a quiet-zone crossing and one that is not. How quiet zones work.

Common questions about trains in Fresno

Do trains run at night in Fresno?

109 of 109 crossings with reported traffic list trains between 6 PM and 6 AM. Check the specific crossing near a home, because night traffic varies by line.

Are there railroad quiet zones in Fresno?

14 public crossings are reported in an FRA quiet zone. Status is reported per crossing, so confirm the crossing nearest the address.

Which railroads operate in Fresno?

By number of public crossings: San Joaquin Valley Railroad (67), BNSF (43), Tulare Valley Railroad (19), Union Pacific (9).

How fast do trains go through Fresno?

The highest maximum timetable speed reported at these crossings is 79 mph. Many urban crossings report much lower limits; the busiest-crossing list shows the speed for each.

How hot does it get in Fresno?

Fresno averages about 113 days a year at 90°F or hotter and 38 at 100°F or hotter, with summer highs near 95°F (NOAA 1991–2020 normals).

Does it freeze or snow in Fresno?

About 11 nights a year drop below freezing, and normal snowfall is 0 inches a year. Winter nights average 41°F.

Sources and limits

Crossing counts come from the FRA Crossing Inventory (Form 71, current), filtered to open, public, at-grade crossings whose inventory city is Fresno (records reported as in or near the city). Incident counts come from FRA highway-rail accident reports (Form 57), January 2021–August 2026. Train counts are railroad-reported estimates with the listed count year; they are not live schedules, noise levels or safety ratings. Read our source coverage and limitations and corrections policy.
