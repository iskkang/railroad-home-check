Common questions about trains in Eugene

Do trains run at night in Eugene?

26 of 27 crossings with reported traffic list trains between 6 PM and 6 AM. Check the specific crossing near a home, because night traffic varies by line.

Are there railroad quiet zones in Eugene?

None of the 35 public crossings in this set is reported in an FRA quiet zone, so routine horn sounding is the default at public crossings.

Which railroads operate in Eugene?

By number of public crossings: Union Pacific (16), Portland & Western Railroad (12), Central Oregon & Pacific Railroad (5), Coos Bay Rail Line (2).

How fast do trains go through Eugene?

The highest maximum timetable speed reported at these crossings is 79 mph. Many urban crossings report much lower limits; the busiest-crossing list shows the speed for each.

Is Eugene a noisy place to live?

About 6% of Eugene residents live where modeled transportation noise averages 55 dB or more over 24 hours (USDOT 2020 noise map), more than the median city among the 299 we cover. Roads are the biggest single source. Noise varies block by block, so check the specific address.

How hot does it get in Eugene?

Eugene averages about 18 days a year at 90°F or hotter and 1 at 100°F or hotter, with summer highs near 81°F (NOAA 1991–2020 normals).

Does it freeze or snow in Eugene?

About 52 nights a year drop below freezing. Winter nights average 35°F.

Sources and limits

Crossing counts come from the FRA Crossing Inventory (Form 71, current), filtered to open, public, at-grade crossings whose inventory city is Eugene (records reported as in or near the city). Incident counts come from FRA highway-rail accident reports (Form 57), January 2021–August 2026. Train counts are railroad-reported estimates with the listed count year; they are not live schedules, noise levels or safety ratings. Read our source coverage and limitations and corrections policy.
