What this means for a home search in Irving

Confirm the crossing, not the neighborhood. Quiet-zone status is reported crossing by crossing (11 of 41 here). Engineers can still sound horns for emergencies, and a home can sit between a quiet-zone crossing and one that is not. How quiet zones work.

The highest counts come from passenger rail. 32 of the 41 crossings are on transit or commuter lines such as DART, Trinity Railway Express, with counts up to 150 trains a day. Railroad-company lines report little traffic here, so the busiest-crossing list includes every operator.

Many counts are old. 19 of 31 active crossings carry a count year before 2021 or none at all (range 2016–2026). Ask the seller or neighbors whether traffic has changed.

Crossing incidents. FRA accident reports list 9 highway-rail incidents at 7 of these crossings between January 2021 and August 2026. That is a safety record for vehicles and pedestrians, not a noise measure. How to read crossing accident history.

Common questions about trains in Irving

Do trains run at night in Irving?

31 of 31 crossings with reported traffic list trains between 6 PM and 6 AM. Check the specific crossing near a home, because night traffic varies by line.

Are there railroad quiet zones in Irving?

11 public crossings are reported in an FRA quiet zone. Status is reported per crossing, so confirm the crossing nearest the address.

Which railroads operate in Irving?

By number of public crossings: DART (17), Trinity Railway Express (15), Fort Worth & Western Railroad (8), BNSF (1).

How fast do trains go through Irving?

The highest maximum timetable speed reported at these crossings is 79 mph. Many urban crossings report much lower limits; the busiest-crossing list shows the speed for each.

Is Irving a noisy place to live?

About 11% of Irving residents live where modeled transportation noise averages 55 dB or more over 24 hours (USDOT 2020 noise map), more than the median city among the 299 we cover. Aircraft are the biggest single source. Noise varies block by block, so check the specific address.

How hot does it get in Irving?

Irving averages about 106 days a year at 90°F or hotter and 20 at 100°F or hotter, with summer highs near 94°F (NOAA 1991–2020 normals).

Does it freeze or snow in Irving?

About 29 nights a year drop below freezing, and normal snowfall is 2 inches a year. Winter nights average 38°F.
