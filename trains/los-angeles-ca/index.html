FRA CROSSING DATA · CA

How Many Trains Run Through Los Angeles, CA?

The FRA crossing inventory lists 171 open public at-grade crossings in or near Los Angeles. 90 report at least one through train per day and 80 report trains between 6 PM and 6 AM. On railroad-company lines (excluding transit agencies), a typical active crossing reports about 3 trains per day; the busiest reports 22.

Data retrieved November 9, 2026 from the FRA crossing inventory (latest record update 2026-10-07).

What this means for a home search in Los Angeles

Night trains are the norm, not the exception. 89% of Los Angeles crossings with reported traffic also report nighttime movements. If the home you are considering sits near any active line, plan at least one visit after dark.

No quiet zones appear in this set. None of the 171 public crossings is reported in an FRA quiet zone, so routine horn sounding at public crossings is the default under the federal Train Horn Rule. Distance to the nearest crossing matters more here than in cities with broad quiet-zone coverage.

The highest counts here come from passenger rail. 49 of the 171 crossings are on transit or commuter lines such as Metrolink (SCRRA), LA Metro. Frequent, shorter trains produce the highest reported counts (up to 208 per day). Railroad-company lines are summarized separately below so the two do not get mixed up.

Many counts are old. 43 of 90 active crossings carry a train-count year before 2021 or no year at all (reported years range from 2002 to 2025). Treat older numbers as a rough guide and ask the seller or neighbors about current traffic.

Crossing incidents. FRA highway-rail accident reports list 40 incidents at 30 of these crossings between January 2021 and August 2026. That is a safety record for vehicles and pedestrians at the crossing, not a noise measure. How to read crossing accident history.

How to check a home near the tracks in Los Angeles

  1. Look up the address and find the nearest public crossing, not just the nearest track.
  2. Note the day and night counts, quiet-zone status and count year for that crossing ID.
  3. Visit once in daytime and once after 10 PM; stand outside and inside with windows closed.
  4. Ask the seller or neighbors whether traffic has changed since the count year.

Common questions about trains in Los Angeles

Do trains run at night in Los Angeles?

Yes on most active lines: 80 of 90 crossings with reported traffic list trains between 6 PM and 6 AM. Check the specific crossing near a home, because night traffic varies by line.

Are there railroad quiet zones in Los Angeles?

None of the 171 public crossings in this set is reported in an FRA quiet zone, so routine horn sounding is the default at public crossings.

Which railroads operate in Los Angeles?

By number of public crossings: Union Pacific (70), Pacific Harbor Line (39), Metrolink (SCRRA) (36), LA Metro (13).

How fast do trains go through Los Angeles?

The highest maximum timetable speed reported at these crossings is 79 mph. Many urban crossings report much lower limits; the table above lists the speed for each busy crossing.

Sources and limits

Crossing counts come from the FRA Crossing Inventory (Form 71, current), filtered to open, public, at-grade crossings whose inventory city is Los Angeles (records reported as in or near the city). Incident counts come from FRA highway-rail accident reports (Form 57), January 2021–August 2026. Train counts are railroad-reported estimates with the listed count year; they are not live schedules, noise levels or safety ratings. Some records omit a count year or use placeholder values.
