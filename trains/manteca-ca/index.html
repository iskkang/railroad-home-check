Common questions about trains in Manteca

Do trains run at night in Manteca?

12 of 12 crossings with reported traffic list trains between 6 PM and 6 AM. Check the specific crossing near a home, because night traffic varies by line.

Are there railroad quiet zones in Manteca?

None of the 17 public crossings in this set is reported in an FRA quiet zone, so routine horn sounding is the default at public crossings.

Which railroads operate in Manteca?

By number of public crossings: Union Pacific (17).

How fast do trains go through Manteca?

The highest maximum timetable speed reported at these crossings is 70 mph. Many urban crossings report much lower limits; the busiest-crossing list shows the speed for each.

Is Manteca a noisy place to live?

About 3% of Manteca residents live where modeled transportation noise averages 55 dB or more over 24 hours (USDOT 2020 noise map), about the same as the median city among the 299 we cover. Rail is the biggest single source. Noise varies block by block, so check the specific address.

How hot does it get in Manteca?

Manteca averages about 88 days a year at 90°F or hotter and 19 at 100°F or hotter, with summer highs near 93°F (NOAA 1991–2020 normals).

Does it freeze or snow in Manteca?

About 19 nights a year drop below freezing. Winter nights average 40°F.
