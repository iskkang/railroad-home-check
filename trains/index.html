What stands out

  • San Antonio, TX has the highest share of crossings reporting night trains (93%).
  • St. Louis, MO has the broadest quiet-zone coverage (96% of public crossings).
  • No public crossing is reported in a quiet zone in Los Angeles, CA, Indianapolis, IN, Detroit, MI, Jacksonville, FL, Philadelphia, PA.

City totals are a starting point. What one house hears depends on the nearest crossing, the line's night traffic and whether horns are restricted there.

How these numbers were built

Records come from the FRA Crossing Inventory (Form 71, current): open, public, at-grade crossings whose inventory city matches each city (including records reported as near the city). Incident totals use FRA Form 57 accident reports from January 2021 to August 2026. Train counts are railroad-reported estimates with varying count years, not schedules. See how to read train counts and data coverage and limits.
