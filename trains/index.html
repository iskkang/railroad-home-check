How these numbers were built

Records come from the FRA Crossing Inventory (Form 71, current): open, public, at-grade crossings whose inventory city matches each city (including records reported as near the city). Incident totals use FRA Form 57 accident reports from January 2021 to August 2026. Train counts are railroad-reported estimates with varying count years, not schedules.
