What this means for a home search in Buffalo

Horns are the default. None of the 23 public crossings is reported in an FRA quiet zone, so routine horn sounding at public crossings applies under the federal Train Horn Rule. Distance to the nearest crossing matters more here than in cities with broad quiet-zone coverage. Train horns near a house.

The highest counts come from passenger rail. 9 of the 23 crossings are on transit or commuter lines such as Niagara Falls Transit Authority, with counts up to 85 trains a day. Railroad-company lines report little traffic here, so the busiest-crossing list includes every operator.

Many counts are old. 9 of 12 active crossings carry a count year before 2021 or none at all (range 2025–2026). Ask the seller or neighbors whether traffic has changed.

Crossing incidents. FRA accident reports list 0 highway-rail incidents at 0 of these crossings between January 2021 and August 2026. That is a safety record for vehicles and pedestrians, not a noise measure. How to read crossing accident history.

Common questions about trains in Buffalo

Do trains run at night in Buffalo?

12 of 12 crossings with reported traffic list trains between 6 PM and 6 AM. Check the specific crossing near a home, because night traffic varies by line.

Are there railroad quiet zones in Buffalo?

None of the 23 public crossings in this set is reported in an FRA quiet zone, so routine horn sounding is the default at public crossings.

Which railroads operate in Buffalo?

By number of public crossings: CSX (10), Niagara Falls Transit Authority (9), Norfolk Southern (4).

How fast do trains go through Buffalo?

The highest maximum timetable speed reported at these crossings is 40 mph. Many urban crossings report much lower limits; the busiest-crossing list shows the speed for each.

Is Buffalo a noisy place to live?

About 4% of Buffalo residents live where modeled transportation noise averages 55 dB or more over 24 hours (USDOT 2020 noise map), more than the median city among the 299 we cover. Roads are the biggest single source. Noise varies block by block, so check the specific address.

How hot does it get in Buffalo?

Buffalo averages about 3 days a year at 90°F or hotter, with summer highs near 78°F (NOAA 1991–2020 normals).

Does it freeze or snow in Buffalo?

About 124 nights a year drop below freezing, and normal snowfall is 95 inches a year. Winter nights average 21°F.

Sources and limits
