Common questions about trains in Modesto

Do trains run at night in Modesto?

48 of 49 crossings with reported traffic list trains between 6 PM and 6 AM. Check the specific crossing near a home, because night traffic varies by line.

Are there railroad quiet zones in Modesto?

None of the 53 public crossings in this set is reported in an FRA quiet zone, so routine horn sounding is the default at public crossings.

Which railroads operate in Modesto?

By number of public crossings: Modesto & Empire Traction (32), Union Pacific (20), BNSF (1).

How fast do trains go through Modesto?

The highest maximum timetable speed reported at these crossings is 79 mph. Many urban crossings report much lower limits; the busiest-crossing list shows the speed for each.

Is Modesto a noisy place to live?

About 1% of Modesto residents live where modeled transportation noise averages 55 dB or more over 24 hours (USDOT 2020 noise map), less than the median city among the 299 we cover. Roads are the biggest single source. Noise varies block by block, so check the specific address.

How hot does it get in Modesto?

Modesto averages about 90 days a year at 90°F or hotter and 18 at 100°F or hotter, with summer highs near 93°F (NOAA 1991–2020 normals).

Does it freeze or snow in Modesto?

About 12 nights a year drop below freezing. Winter nights average 40°F.

Sources and limits
