FRA CROSSING DATA · CA

How Many Trains Run Through San Diego, CA?

The FRA crossing inventory lists 123 open public at-grade crossings in or near San Diego. 105 report at least one through train per day and 100 report trains between 6 PM and 6 AM. Most of that activity is light rail and commuter service: a typical active crossing reports about 120 trains per day, and the busiest reports 214. Only 11 crossings on railroad-company lines report daily traffic.

Data retrieved November 9, 2026 from the FRA crossing inventory (latest record update 2026-10-30).

What this means for a home search in San Diego

Night trains are the norm, not the exception. 95% of San Diego crossings with reported traffic also report nighttime movements. If the home you are considering sits near any active line, plan at least one visit after dark.

Quiet zones are patchy. Only 12 of 123 public crossings (10%) are reported in an FRA quiet zone. Two homes a few blocks apart can have very different horn exposure, so check the crossing closest to each address. How quiet zones work.

The highest counts here come from passenger rail. 98 of the 123 crossings are on transit or commuter lines such as San Diego Trolley, San Diego MTS, NCTD Coaster. Frequent, shorter trains produce the highest reported counts (up to 214 per day). Railroad-company lines report little traffic in this set, so the table below lists the busiest crossings of any operator.

Many counts are old. 87 of 105 active crossings carry a train-count year before 2021 or no year at all (reported years range from 2000 to 2026). Treat older numbers as a rough guide and ask the seller or neighbors about current traffic.

Crossing incidents. FRA highway-rail accident reports list 39 incidents at 24 of these crossings between January 2021 and August 2026. That is a safety record for vehicles and pedestrians at the crossing, not a noise measure. How to read crossing accident history.

How to check a home near the tracks in San Diego

  1. Look up the address and find the nearest public crossing, not just the nearest track.
  2. Note the day and night counts, quiet-zone status and count year for that crossing ID.
  3. Visit once in daytime and once after 10 PM; stand outside and inside with windows closed.
  4. Ask the seller or neighbors whether traffic has changed since the count year.

Common questions about trains in San Diego

Do trains run at night in San Diego?

Yes on most active lines: 100 of 105 crossings with reported traffic list trains between 6 PM and 6 AM. Check the specific crossing near a home, because night traffic varies by line.

Are there railroad quiet zones in San Diego?

12 public crossings are reported in an FRA quiet zone. Status is reported per crossing, so confirm the crossing nearest the address.

Which railroads operate in San Diego?

By number of public crossings: San Diego Trolley (58), San Diego MTS (20), BNSF (20), NCTD Coaster (18).

How fast do trains go through San Diego?

The highest maximum timetable speed reported at these crossings is 65 mph. Many urban crossings report much lower limits; the table above lists the speed for each busy crossing.

Sources and limits

Crossing counts come from the FRA Crossing Inventory (Form 71, current), filtered to open, public, at-grade crossings whose inventory city is San Diego (records reported as in or near the city). Incident counts come from FRA highway-rail accident reports (Form 57), January 2021–August 2026. Train counts are railroad-reported estimates with the listed count year; they are not live schedules, noise levels or safety ratings. Some records omit a count year or use placeholder values.
