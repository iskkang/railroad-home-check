Common questions about trains in Tucson

Do trains run at night in Tucson?

25 of 25 crossings with reported traffic list trains between 6 PM and 6 AM. Check the specific crossing near a home, because night traffic varies by line.

Are there railroad quiet zones in Tucson?

None of the 28 public crossings in this set is reported in an FRA quiet zone, so routine horn sounding is the default at public crossings.

Which railroads operate in Tucson?

By number of public crossings: Union Pacific (28).

How fast do trains go through Tucson?

The highest maximum timetable speed reported at these crossings is 79 mph. Many urban crossings report much lower limits; the busiest-crossing list shows the speed for each.

How hot does it get in Tucson?

Tucson averages about 158 days a year at 90°F or hotter and 68 at 100°F or hotter, with summer highs near 100°F (NOAA 1991–2020 normals).

Does it freeze or snow in Tucson?

About 12 nights a year drop below freezing, and normal snowfall is 0 inches a year. Winter nights average 42°F.
