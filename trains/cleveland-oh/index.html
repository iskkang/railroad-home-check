What this means for a home search in Cleveland

Horns are the default. None of the 73 public crossings is reported in an FRA quiet zone, so routine horn sounding at public crossings applies under the federal Train Horn Rule. Distance to the nearest crossing matters more here than in cities with broad quiet-zone coverage. Train horns near a house.

Many counts are old. 27 of 50 active crossings carry a count year before 2021 or none at all (range 2017–2026). Ask the seller or neighbors whether traffic has changed.

Crossing incidents. FRA accident reports list 7 highway-rail incidents at 5 of these crossings between January 2021 and August 2026. That is a safety record for vehicles and pedestrians, not a noise measure. How to read crossing accident history.

Common questions about trains in Cleveland

Do trains run at night in Cleveland?

34 of 50 crossings with reported traffic list trains between 6 PM and 6 AM. Check the specific crossing near a home, because night traffic varies by line.

Are there railroad quiet zones in Cleveland?

None of the 73 public crossings in this set is reported in an FRA quiet zone, so routine horn sounding is the default at public crossings.

Which railroads operate in Cleveland?

By number of public crossings: Norfolk Southern (27), CSX (17), Cleveland & Cuyahoga Railway (13), Greater Cleveland Regional Transit Authority (5).

How fast do trains go through Cleveland?

The highest maximum timetable speed reported at these crossings is 70 mph. Many urban crossings report much lower limits; the busiest-crossing list shows the speed for each.

Is Cleveland a noisy place to live?

About 5% of Cleveland residents live where modeled transportation noise averages 55 dB or more over 24 hours (USDOT 2020 noise map), more than the median city among the 299 we cover. Aircraft are the biggest single source. Noise varies block by block, so check the specific address.

How hot does it get in Cleveland?

Cleveland averages about 9 days a year at 90°F or hotter, with summer highs near 79°F (NOAA 1991–2020 normals).

Does it freeze or snow in Cleveland?

About 92 nights a year drop below freezing. Winter nights average 26°F.

Sources and limits

Crossing counts come from the FRA Crossing Inventory (Form 71, current), filtered to open, public, at-grade crossings whose inventory city is Cleveland (records reported as in or near the city). Incident counts come from FRA highway-rail accident reports (Form 57), January 2021–August 2026. Train counts are railroad-reported estimates with the listed count year; they are not live schedules, noise levels or safety ratings. Read our source coverage and limitations and corrections policy.
