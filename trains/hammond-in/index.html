What this means for a home search in Hammond

Confirm the crossing, not the neighborhood. Quiet-zone status is reported crossing by crossing (8 of 59 here). Engineers can still sound horns for emergencies, and a home can sit between a quiet-zone crossing and one that is not. How quiet zones work.

The highest counts come from passenger rail. 14 of the 59 crossings are on transit or commuter lines such as South Shore Line (NICTD), with counts up to 62 trains a day. Railroad-company lines are listed separately below.

Crossing incidents. FRA accident reports list 27 highway-rail incidents at 18 of these crossings between January 2021 and August 2026. That is a safety record for vehicles and pedestrians, not a noise measure. How to read crossing accident history.

Common questions about trains in Hammond

Do trains run at night in Hammond?

45 of 47 crossings with reported traffic list trains between 6 PM and 6 AM. Check the specific crossing near a home, because night traffic varies by line.

Are there railroad quiet zones in Hammond?

8 public crossings are reported in an FRA quiet zone. Status is reported per crossing, so confirm the crossing nearest the address.

Which railroads operate in Hammond?

By number of public crossings: Norfolk Southern (22), South Shore Line (NICTD) (14), CSX (10), Indiana Harbor Belt Railroad (10).

How fast do trains go through Hammond?

The highest maximum timetable speed reported at these crossings is 79 mph. Many urban crossings report much lower limits; the busiest-crossing list shows the speed for each.

Is Hammond a noisy place to live?

About 17% of Hammond residents live where modeled transportation noise averages 55 dB or more over 24 hours (USDOT 2020 noise map), more than the median city among the 299 we cover. Roads are the biggest single source. Noise varies block by block, so check the specific address.

How hot does it get in Hammond?

Hammond averages about 16 days a year at 90°F or hotter and 0 at 100°F or hotter, with summer highs near 82°F (NOAA 1991–2020 normals).

Does it freeze or snow in Hammond?

About 126 nights a year drop below freezing, and normal snowfall is 28 inches a year. Winter nights average 18°F.
