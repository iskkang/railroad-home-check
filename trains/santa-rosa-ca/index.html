What this means for a home search in Santa Rosa

Confirm the crossing, not the neighborhood. Quiet-zone status is reported crossing by crossing (17 of 21 here). Engineers can still sound horns for emergencies, and a home can sit between a quiet-zone crossing and one that is not. How quiet zones work.

The highest counts come from passenger rail. 21 of the 21 crossings are on transit or commuter lines such as Sonoma Marin Area Rail Transit, with counts up to 42 trains a day. Railroad-company lines report little traffic here, so the busiest-crossing list includes every operator.

Crossing incidents. FRA accident reports list 3 highway-rail incidents at 3 of these crossings between January 2021 and August 2026. That is a safety record for vehicles and pedestrians, not a noise measure. How to read crossing accident history.

Common questions about trains in Santa Rosa

Do trains run at night in Santa Rosa?

17 of 17 crossings with reported traffic list trains between 6 PM and 6 AM. Check the specific crossing near a home, because night traffic varies by line.

Are there railroad quiet zones in Santa Rosa?

17 public crossings are reported in an FRA quiet zone. Status is reported per crossing, so confirm the crossing nearest the address.

Which railroads operate in Santa Rosa?

By number of public crossings: Sonoma Marin Area Rail Transit (21).

How fast do trains go through Santa Rosa?

The highest maximum timetable speed reported at these crossings is 79 mph. Many urban crossings report much lower limits; the busiest-crossing list shows the speed for each.

How hot does it get in Santa Rosa?

Santa Rosa averages about 34 days a year at 90°F or hotter and 4 at 100°F or hotter, with summer highs near 83°F (NOAA 1991–2020 normals).

Does it freeze or snow in Santa Rosa?

About 31 nights a year drop below freezing. Winter nights average 38°F.
