Common questions about trains in Waco

Do trains run at night in Waco?

31 of 31 crossings with reported traffic list trains between 6 PM and 6 AM. Check the specific crossing near a home, because night traffic varies by line.

Are there railroad quiet zones in Waco?

None of the 34 public crossings in this set is reported in an FRA quiet zone, so routine horn sounding is the default at public crossings.

Which railroads operate in Waco?

By number of public crossings: Union Pacific (34).

How fast do trains go through Waco?

The highest maximum timetable speed reported at these crossings is 60 mph. Many urban crossings report much lower limits; the busiest-crossing list shows the speed for each.

Is Waco a noisy place to live?

About 1% of Waco residents live where modeled transportation noise averages 55 dB or more over 24 hours (USDOT 2020 noise map), less than the median city among the 299 we cover. Roads are the biggest single source. Noise varies block by block, so check the specific address.

How hot does it get in Waco?

Waco averages about 115 days a year at 90°F or hotter and 24 at 100°F or hotter, with summer highs near 96°F (NOAA 1991–2020 normals).

Does it freeze or snow in Waco?

About 34 nights a year drop below freezing, and normal snowfall is 1 inches a year. Winter nights average 38°F.
