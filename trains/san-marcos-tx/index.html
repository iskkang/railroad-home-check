What this means for a home search in San Marcos

Confirm the crossing, not the neighborhood. Quiet-zone status is reported crossing by crossing (24 of 33 here). Engineers can still sound horns for emergencies, and a home can sit between a quiet-zone crossing and one that is not. How quiet zones work.

Crossing incidents. FRA accident reports list 5 highway-rail incidents at 4 of these crossings between January 2021 and August 2026. That is a safety record for vehicles and pedestrians, not a noise measure. How to read crossing accident history.

Common questions about trains in San Marcos

Do trains run at night in San Marcos?

33 of 33 crossings with reported traffic list trains between 6 PM and 6 AM. Check the specific crossing near a home, because night traffic varies by line.

Are there railroad quiet zones in San Marcos?

24 public crossings are reported in an FRA quiet zone. Status is reported per crossing, so confirm the crossing nearest the address.

Which railroads operate in San Marcos?

By number of public crossings: Union Pacific (33).

How fast do trains go through San Marcos?

The highest maximum timetable speed reported at these crossings is 70 mph. Many urban crossings report much lower limits; the busiest-crossing list shows the speed for each.

How hot does it get in San Marcos?

San Marcos averages about 128 days a year at 90°F or hotter and 26 at 100°F or hotter, with summer highs near 96°F (NOAA 1991–2020 normals).

Does it freeze or snow in San Marcos?

About 20 nights a year drop below freezing. Winter nights average 42°F.

Sources and limits

Crossing counts come from the FRA Crossing Inventory (Form 71, current), filtered to open, public, at-grade crossings whose inventory city is San Marcos (records reported as in or near the city).
