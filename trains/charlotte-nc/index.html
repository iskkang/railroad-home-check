FRA CROSSING DATA · NC

How Many Trains Run Through Charlotte, NC?

The FRA crossing inventory lists 139 open public at-grade crossings in or near Charlotte. 64 report at least one through train per day and 56 report trains between 6 PM and 6 AM. On railroad-company lines (excluding transit agencies), a typical active crossing reports about 4 trains per day; the busiest reports 28.

What this means for a home search in Charlotte

Night trains are the norm, not the exception. 88% of Charlotte crossings with reported traffic also report nighttime movements. If the home you are considering sits near any active line, plan at least one visit after dark.

Quiet zones are patchy. Only 2 of 139 public crossings (1%) are reported in an FRA quiet zone. Two homes a few blocks apart can have very different horn exposure, so check the crossing closest to each address. How quiet zones work.

Most counts are recent. 64 of 64 active crossings report a train-count year of 2021 or later (range 2023–2026). Counts are still reported estimates, not schedules.

Crossing incidents. FRA highway-rail accident reports list 31 incidents at 21 of these crossings between January 2021 and August 2026. That is a safety record for vehicles and pedestrians at the crossing, not a noise measure. How to read crossing accident history.

How to check a home near the tracks in Charlotte

  1. Look up the address and find the nearest public crossing, not just the nearest track.
  2. Note the day and night counts, quiet-zone status and count year for that crossing ID.
  3. Visit once in daytime and once after 10 PM; stand outside and inside with windows closed.
  4. Ask the seller or neighbors whether traffic has changed since the count year.

Common questions about trains in Charlotte

Do trains run at night in Charlotte?

Yes on most active lines: 56 of 64 crossings with reported traffic list trains between 6 PM and 6 AM. Check the specific crossing near a home, because night traffic varies by line.

Are there railroad quiet zones in Charlotte?

2 public crossings are reported in an FRA quiet zone. Status is reported per crossing, so confirm the crossing nearest the address.

Which railroads operate in Charlotte?

By number of public crossings: Norfolk Southern (76), CSX (48), Aberdeen, Carolina & Western Railroad (12).

How fast do trains go through Charlotte?

The highest maximum timetable speed reported at these crossings is 79 mph. Many urban crossings report much lower limits; the table above lists the speed for each busy crossing.

Sources and limits

Crossing counts come from the FRA Crossing Inventory (Form 71, current), filtered to open, public, at-grade crossings whose inventory city is Charlotte (records reported as in or near the city). Incident counts come from FRA highway-rail accident reports (Form 57), January 2021–August 2026. Train counts are railroad-reported estimates with the listed count year; they are not live schedules, noise levels or safety ratings. Some records omit a count year or use placeholder values.
