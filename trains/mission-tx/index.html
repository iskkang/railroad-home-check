What this means for a home search in Mission

Horns are the default. None of the 48 public crossings is reported in an FRA quiet zone, so routine horn sounding at public crossings applies under the federal Train Horn Rule. Distance to the nearest crossing matters more here than in cities with broad quiet-zone coverage. Train horns near a house.

Many counts are old. 26 of 26 active crossings carry a count year before 2021 or none at all (range 2019–2019). Ask the seller or neighbors whether traffic has changed.

Common questions about trains in Mission

Do trains run at night in Mission?

23 of 26 crossings with reported traffic list trains between 6 PM and 6 AM. Check the specific crossing near a home, because night traffic varies by line.

Are there railroad quiet zones in Mission?

None of the 48 public crossings in this set is reported in an FRA quiet zone, so routine horn sounding is the default at public crossings.

Which railroads operate in Mission?

By number of public crossings: Rio Valley Switching (44), Border Pacific Railroad (4).

How fast do trains go through Mission?

The highest maximum timetable speed reported at these crossings is 30 mph. Many urban crossings report much lower limits; the busiest-crossing list shows the speed for each.

How hot does it get in Mission?

Mission averages about 177 days a year at 90°F or hotter and 40 at 100°F or hotter, with summer highs near 99°F (NOAA 1991–2020 normals).

Does it freeze or snow in Mission?

About 1 nights a year drop below freezing. Winter nights average 55°F.
