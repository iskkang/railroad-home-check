Common questions about trains in Davenport

Do trains run at night in Davenport?

24 of 25 crossings with reported traffic list trains between 6 PM and 6 AM. Check the specific crossing near a home, because night traffic varies by line.

Are there railroad quiet zones in Davenport?

None of the 37 public crossings in this set is reported in an FRA quiet zone, so routine horn sounding is the default at public crossings.

Which railroads operate in Davenport?

By number of public crossings: Dakota, Minnesota & Eastern (CPKC) (25), Iowa Interstate Railroad (12).

How fast do trains go through Davenport?

The highest maximum timetable speed reported at these crossings is 40 mph. Many urban crossings report much lower limits; the busiest-crossing list shows the speed for each.

Sources and limits

Crossing counts come from the FRA Crossing Inventory (Form 71, current), filtered to open, public, at-grade crossings whose inventory city is Davenport (records reported as in or near the city).
