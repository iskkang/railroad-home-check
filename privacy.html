PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

This policy explains how Railroad Home Check processes property addresses, measures site usage, and uses advertising services. The service is an independent informational tool for U.S. home buyers.

Property addresses

A property address is used only to locate the property and generate the requested railroad report. It is sent to the U.S. Census Geocoder for location matching and then used to query nearby Federal Railroad Administration records.

Railroad Home Check does not store property addresses in a database, sell them, reuse them for marketing, or include them in Analytics event parameters. Ordinary searches send addresses to our geocoding endpoint in a POST request body. If you choose to share, the copied link includes the matched address in its URL fragment. Anyone with that link can read the address. On opening the page, we read and remove that fragment before loading analytics and advertising scripts. The recipient must select the search button to run a new report.

Maps, comparisons, and printed reports

Comparison reports are held in this browser tab's memory, not in a server database or browser persistent storage. Refreshing or closing the tab clears them. Printed or saved PDF reports contain the displayed addresses and results.

Interactive maps and surrounding road/rail data load automatically after a successful address search. Map tiles are provided by OpenStreetMap, whose servers receive your IP address and the area of the map requested. Property addresses are not sent in map tile requests. Approximate property coordinates are sent to ArcGIS services to retrieve road traffic and rail geometry. See the OpenStreetMap Foundation privacy policy.

Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand aggregate site traffic and whether property checks start, complete, or fail. Google Analytics may use cookies and similar technologies to collect information such as page views, device and browser information, and general interaction data.

Our custom Analytics events do not include the entered address, matched address, ZIP code, city, coordinates, or error text. Google processes Analytics data under its own terms and privacy practices.

Google AdSense and advertising cookies

We use Google AdSense to serve and measure advertising. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a visitor's prior visits to this website or other websites.

Google's use of advertising cookies enables Google and its partners to serve ads based on visits to this site and other sites on the Internet. Other third-party vendors or ad networks may also serve ads and use cookies or similar technologies where enabled.

Learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services in Google's partner-sites policy.

Your choices

  • Manage or opt out of personalized Google advertising through Google Ads Settings.
  • Use the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on where available.
  • Control or delete cookies through your browser settings. Blocking cookies may affect some site or advertising functions.
  • Visitors in regions that require consent may be shown additional privacy and advertising choices.

External data services and links

The service uses data or processing from the U.S. Census Bureau, the U.S. Department of Transportation, and the Federal Railroad Administration. External websites and services operate under their own privacy policies.

Children's privacy

Railroad Home Check is intended for home-buyer due diligence and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly request personal information from children.

Changes and questions

We may update this policy when the service or its providers change. The updated date at the top of this page will show the latest revision.

Questions may be submitted through the project's GitHub issue tracker. Do not post property addresses or other sensitive information in a public issue.

Railroad Home Check is an independent informational tool and is not affiliated with the FRA, DOT, a railroad, a real-estate broker, or a home inspector.
